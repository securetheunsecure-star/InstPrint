Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find a nearby printer?

Enter your postal code or location in the search box on the "Find a Printer" page. You'll see available printer owners nearby with their prices and availability.

How much can I earn as a printer owner?

Earnings depend on your prices and order volume. InstPrint charges a 20% commission on printing subtotal (not including the service fee). You keep 80% of the printing revenue.

What payment methods do you accept?

This is a prototype. Demo checkout simulates payment but does not process real transactions. A production version would support multiple payment methods.

Is my data secure?

This prototype stores data in your browser's localStorage. It is not suitable for production use. Never enter real payment credentials. A production system would use secure servers and encryption.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes. If an order is in "Pending" or "Paid" status, you can cancel it from your order history. Once printing has started, cancellation may not be possible.

How does the distance calculation work?

This prototype uses postal codes and illustrative distance data for demonstration. Real implementation would use precise coordinates and actual distance calculations.
