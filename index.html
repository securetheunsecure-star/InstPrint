Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find a nearby printer?

Enter a postal code or nearby area on the Find a Printer page to compare home-based print providers.

How much can I earn as a printer owner?

It depends on your set per-page rates. InstPrint's prototype uses a 20% commission on the printing subtotal.

Do you process real payments?

No. This prototype uses a simulated demo checkout and does not process real payment details.

Is the listing shared publicly?

No. LocalStorage is browser-specific. Your printer listing is not published to other users or devices.
